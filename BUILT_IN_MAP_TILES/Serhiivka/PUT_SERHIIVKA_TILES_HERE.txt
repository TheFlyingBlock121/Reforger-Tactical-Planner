DROP THE SERHIIVKA PNG TILES DIRECTLY INTO THIS FOLDER.

Expected naming:
  tile_r000_c000.png  = TOP LEFT
  tile_r000_c001.png  = one tile to the RIGHT
  tile_r001_c000.png  = one tile DOWN

Rows increase TOP -> BOTTOM.
Columns increase LEFT -> RIGHT.

Do not rename or stitch the tiles.
The build script detects the grid size and tile resolution automatically.
